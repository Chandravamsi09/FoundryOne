# FoundryOne Employee Leave Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the standard leave entitlements and guidelines for FoundryOne employees.

### Entitlements
1. **Paid Time Off (PTO)**: 20 days per calendar year.
2. **Sick Leave**: 10 days per calendar year.
3. **Maternity / Paternity Leave**: As per local labor regulations.
4. **Casual Leave**: 5 days per year.

### Application Process
- Applications must be submitted via the Employee Portal.
- Advance notice of at least 3 business days is requested for non-emergency leaves.
- Approvals are handled by the designated reporting manager.
